\begin{frame}[label=fractional]{Fractional regularization keeps the proof and allows jumps}
\small
Replace $\alpha^2\norm{\nabla\vct}_{L^2}^2$ by $\alpha^2[\vct]_s^2$, $0<s<1$, on $H^s(\Omega;\R^2)$:
\[[\vct]_s^2=\int_\Omega\!\int_\Omega\frac{|\vct(\mathbf x)-\vct(\mathbf x')|^2}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|^{2+2s}}\,d\mathbf x\,d\mathbf x'.\]
\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
% source: |x-x'|<=D on Omega x Omega and the identity int int |w(x)-w(x')|^2 = 2|Omega| ||w||^2 for mean-zero w.
\item \textbf{Same null space, explicit Poincar\'e:} if $\int_\Omega\mathbf w=0$ and $D=\operatorname{diam}\Omega$, then $\norm{\mathbf w}_{L^2}^2\le\frac{D^{2+2s}}{2|\Omega|}[\mathbf w]_s^2$.
\item \textbf{Same proof:} the lemma with $m=\lambda_{\min}(\int_\Omega\B)>0$ gives $H^s$ coercivity, Lax--Milgram, and stability in $H^s$.
\item \textbf{Motion boundaries:} on the image rectangle the $H^1$ minimizer lies in $H^2\subset C(\bar\Omega)$; piecewise-constant motion lies in $H^s$ exactly when $s<\tfrac12$.
\item \textbf{Discrete:} all-pairs weights $w_{ij}\propto|\mathbf x_i-\mathbf x_j|^{-2-2s}$ give a connected graph: block-Jacobi converges, with dense sweeps.
\end{itemize}
\takeaway{The proof needs a quadratic seminorm that vanishes only on constants and has a Poincar\'e inequality; fractional seminorms supply both and, for $s<\frac12$, allow jumps.}

The coercivity constant has the $H^1$ form with $C_P^2$ replaced by $D^{2+2s}/(2|\Omega|)$. Regularity: $\Delta\vct^*=(\B\vct^*-b)/\alpha^2\in L^2$ with the natural Neumann condition, and Neumann $H^2$ regularity on a convex polygon (Grisvard) gives $\vct^*\in H^2$, continuous in two dimensions.}{Jumps: for $\vct=\vct_1\chi_E+\vct_2\chi_{\Omega\setminus E}$ with $E$ of finite perimeter, $[\vct]_s^2=2|\vct_1-\vct_2|^2\int_E\int_{\Omega\setminus E}|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|^{-2-2s}$, finite for $s<\frac12$ and infinite for $s\ge\frac12$ on a connected domain. The penalty is quadratic in the jump size, while TV is linear in it, so jumps are admissible but still shrunk. Euler--Lagrange operator: the regional fractional Laplacian; Warma (2015) treats its Neumann-type boundary conditions. A spectral variant $\sum_k\mu_k^s|\hat v_k|^2$ also vanishes only on constants; Antil--Bartels use spectral fractional operators in imaging.}
